How this works. This page lists the routines currently running. It cannot switch them off by itself. Tap Copy on any routine, paste the message to Muse, and Muse will switch it off and update this list. Every time a routine is added or changed, this list is updated too. Paused routines are not listed here. Ask Muse to resume one.
